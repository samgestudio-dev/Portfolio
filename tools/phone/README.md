# iPhone Air, the reference

`iphone-air.glb` is "iPhone Air" by **Ranguel** on Sketchfab
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-air-055022b853aa4c6cb25cc6979178e719),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). A non-official, fan-made model.

The page does not load it. `src/phone/scene.js` rebuilds the phone as Hairline solids
(rounded prisms: a silhouette and one crease) at this model's proportions and parts:
the camera plateau and its lens, the flash and microphone, the buttons, the glass. The
inside (board, chips, battery, Taptic Engine, speaker) follows iFixit's teardown.
