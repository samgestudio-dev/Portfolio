# iPhone Air

`iphone-air.glb` is "iPhone Air" by **Ranguel** on Sketchfab
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/iphone-air-055022b853aa4c6cb25cc6979178e719),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). A non-official, fan-made model.
It is used here as it came; nothing in the file was changed.

The page (`src/phone/`) adds what the model does not have: the logic board with its chips,
the battery, the Taptic Engine and the speaker, laid out after iFixit's teardown (the board
under the camera plateau, the battery filling the rest), and the interface on the screen.
